Frequently Asked

Who can participate?

Any currently enrolled student at IWU or neighboring colleges! We welcome all majors—not just CS. Diversity in thinking builds better solutions.

What if I can't code?

Perfect! Hackathons need designers, writers, business thinkers, and subject matter experts. We'll have workshops to help you get started.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, chargers, a refillable water bottle, and your creative energy. If you're staying overnight, maybe a blanket or sleeping bag!

Is there a cost?

Nope. HackTitan is 100% free for students thanks to our amazing sponsors. We even provide all your meals and snacks.
